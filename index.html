Procedure Guidance: The Right Way to Make a 1L Solution

Step 1: Weigh and Dissolve the Solute

Accurately weigh your solute and transfer it to a beaker with a smaller amount of solvent (e.g., ~700 mL for a 1L solution). Stir until fully dissolved. Never add solute directly to a full liter of solvent!

Step 2: Transfer to a Volumetric Flask

Carefully pour the dissolved solution into a 1L volumetric flask. Rinse the beaker with a small amount of solvent and add the rinsing to the flask to ensure no solute is lost.

Step 3: Dilute to the Mark

Add solvent to the flask until the bottom of the meniscus reaches the calibration mark on the neck. Use a dropper for the final drops to ensure precision.

Step 4: Mix Thoroughly

Stopper the flask and invert it several times to ensure the solution is homogeneous.
